Read this before the numbers

Both pooled subjects are libraries — one Go, one C#. The corpus is designed around size × application type; this covers a single cell of that grid at two sizes. Four of the five measurable tools have overlapping precision ranges. This is a preliminary result from a pilot whose purpose was to validate the instrument, not to rank the field.

What was measured

Each tool reviewed the same two pull requests — prometheus/prometheus#18081 and dotnet/efcore#34127 — twice, from a local checkout pinned to the commit the first human reviewer saw, with network access time-boxed so no tool could read the answer. Every finding from all fourteen cells was pooled, clustered, and graded blind by a judge that never learned which tool produced which claim.

Precision is the share of a tool's reported findings the judge called substantive. Thread recall is the share of the human reviewers' own comments the tool independently raised — the only axis that measures whether the bug was found rather than whether the claim was defensible.

Signal vs noise — the split precision hides

Precision collapses four graded outcomes into two. These tools almost never emit a false positive; what separates them is how much correct-but-immaterial output they add.

Four things the data supports

01

No tool wins on every axis

comprehensive-review leads on precision. ours-audit finds the most real defects and the most that nobody else found, at the worst precision and the highest cost. superpowers is close to both on substance at a seventh of ours-audit's spend.

02

The quiet tools are right, and they miss almost everything

anthropic-code-review reported eleven findings across both subjects and all eleven were real — a perfect record with no ratio attached, because eleven splits into seven and four. Both withheld tools sit at the bottom of thread recall: 0.15 and 0.05.

03

False positives do not separate the field

One false positive across all twenty-eight pooled cells, and three across all 793 findings in the pilot. These tools do not invent defects. They fail by immateriality — trivia and true-but-trivial observations — not by error.

04

Precision is not detection

On a third subject chosen for having no known defect, precision ranged 0.38–0.83 — overlapping the 0.46–1.00 seen where real bugs existed. Precision measures whether a claim is defensible, not whether the bug was found. Only thread recall answers that, and there the spread runs 0.05 to 0.60.

What this may not be used for

  • Any claim about application types other than library. Both pooled subjects are libraries.
  • A ranking presented as settled. Two subjects, and four of five ranges overlap.
  • A precision figure for anthropic-code-review or ours-bugs — withheld deliberately, not missing.
  • Naming which human review threads the field missed. Across grading passes the count reproduces and the identity does not.
  • Pooling with the null subject or with any in-window subject — the aggregator refuses.
